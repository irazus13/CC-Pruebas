## Resumen

**OBJECTIVE:** To evaluate AI-based identification of C-shaped canals across dental radiographic modalities, with an exploratory synthesis of conventional panoramic detection using cone-beam computed tomography (CBCT) reference labels. **METHODS:** Seven sources were searched through August 12, 2026. Peer-reviewed original studies were appraised using QUADAS-3, STARD-AI, and GRADE. Binary panoramic estimates with CBCT reference and auditable counts were pooled using a Bayesian bivariate model nesting external centers within parent studies. **PROSPERO:** CRD420251046384. **RESULTS:** Nine studies were included; three independent parent studies supplied five cohorts (899C-shaped and 953 non-C-shaped teeth). Pooled sensitivity was 0.918 (95&#x202f;% credible interval [CrI], 0.837-0.962) and specificity 0.885 (0.588-0.969). Marginal model-implied new-parent-study prediction intervals were 0.716-0.981 and 0.219-0.993, respectively. Only one program provided multicenter external evaluation, with center-specific optimized thresholds. All selected estimates had high risk of bias. Certainty was low for sensitivity and very low for specificity. **CONCLUSIONS:** The sparse evidence does not establish dependable clinical performance. Predictive uncertainty and workflow limitations constrain transportability. Prospective evaluation of prespecified systems and clinical consequences is needed before implementation.
