## Resumen

This study aimed to develop and evaluate a generalized deep convolutional neural network (DCNN) model, ApexNet-17, for predicting endodontic outcomes across all tooth types using preoperative periapical radiographs. A dataset comprising 2,603 teeth from two institutions was utilized. Endodontic outcomes were determined based on tooth survival and radiographic periapical health during 1-3&#xa0;years of follow-up using the periapical index (PAI). A 17-layer DCNN incorporating self-attention, attention pooling, and five radiographic features was trained and internally validated using 2,279 cases and externally tested using 324 cases. Gradient-weighted class activation mapping (Grad-CAM) was applied to visualize regions contributing to model predictions. ApexNet-17 achieved an accuracy of 72.1%, a sensitivity of 58.5%, and an AUC of 0.666-performance that, while improved over the pilot model, remains below the level typically considered acceptable for clinical decision support. Compared with the previous premolar-only model, ApexNet-17 showed statistically higher accuracy and sensitivity (p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.05) but substantially lower specificity (50.6% vs. 78.3%), indicating a higher false-positive rate. Because the models were evaluated using different datasets, these comparisons should be interpreted cautiously, and statistical significance should not be regarded as evidence of clinical superiority. Limitations include the exclusion of intermediate PAI scores (2 and 3), the number and characteristics of which could not be assessed retrospectively, and the uncertain performance of the model in clinically ambiguous cases. Further refinement and validation are required before clinical implementation, although the findings support the feasibility of AI-based prediction of endodontic outcomes using preoperative periapical radiographs.
