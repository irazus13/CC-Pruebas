## Resumen

The 4th West Asia and North Africa (WANA) International Congress, held on May 3-4, 2025 in Kish Island (Iran), was a significant event organized by the Royan Institute in collaboration with domestic and international partners. It gathered the leading experts in stem cell research, regenerative medicine and industry to discuss the latest advances in these fields, and strategize overcoming scientific and clinical challenges specific to the WANA region. The congress showcased a dynamic mix of plenary sessions, workshops, and exhibitions highlighting cutting-edge innovations from science-based companies, fostering international collaboration and knowledge exchange. Key Highlights included breakthroughs in liver regenerative medicine using adult stem/progenitor cells, dopaminergic progenitor cell transplantation for Parkinson disease, stem cell-mediated hearing regeneration, and novel approaches to osteoarthritis (OA) and cancer therapy. The event also emphasized the transformative role of artificial intelligence (AI) and computational biology in drug discovery, research transparency, and personalized therapies through multi-omics profiling. This article highlights the congress scientific initiatives in stem cell research, showcasing recent advancements, new opportunities, and obstacles in turning research into clinical practice, while highlighting the importance of regional cooperation in fostering innovation within regenerative medicine.
