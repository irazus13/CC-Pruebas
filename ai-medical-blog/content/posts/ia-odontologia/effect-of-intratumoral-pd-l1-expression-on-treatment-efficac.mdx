## Resumen

The aim of this study was to clarify the association between programmed death-ligand 1 (PD-L1) expression and the treatment efficacy of anaplastic lymphoma kinase (ALK)-tyrosine kinase inhibitors (TKIs), particularly alectinib, in patients with advanced or recurrent ALK-positive non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC). This multicenter retrospective cohort study involved 129 patients with advanced or recurrent ALK-positive NSCLC treated between July 2014 and December 2022 at 13 Japanese institutions. All patients had available PD-L1 tumor proportion score (TPS) data evaluated using the 22C3 antibody and received at least one ALK-TKI. The primary endpoint was progression-free survival (PFS) with the first ALK-TKI. The secondary endpoints were overall survival (OS), objective response rate (ORR), and incidence of grade&#x2009;&#x2265;&#x2009;3 adverse events (AEs). The low- (&lt;&#x2009;50%, n&#x2009;=&#x2009;74) and high-PD-L1 groups (&#x2265;&#x2009;50%, n&#x2009;=&#x2009;55) showed comparable ORRs (86.5%, 95% confidence interval [CI]: 76.5%-93.3% vs. 85.5%, 95% CI: 73.3%-93.5%; p&#x2009;=&#x2009;1.00), median PFS (29.7, 95% CI: 18.8-51.2 months vs. 35.0, 95% CI: 16.6-54.2 months; hazard ratio [HR]: 1.01, 95% CI: 0.63-1.63; p&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.961), and median OS (119.9, 95% CI: 60.1 months to NA vs. 76.6, 95% CI: 43.0 months to NA; HR: 1.74, 95% CI: 0.91-3.32; p&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.095). PD-L1 expression was not significantly associated with survival. However, severe (grade&#x2009;&#x2265;&#x2009;3) AEs were more common in the high-PD-L1 group than in the low-PD-L1 group (21.8% vs. 5.4%). No patients received concomitant cytotoxic chemotherapy or immune checkpoint inhibitors during treatment with the first ALK-TKI, and the frequency of prior systemic therapy before the first ALK-TKI was similar between the two PD-L1 groups. Intratumoral PD-L1 expression was not significantly associated with the efficacy of ALK-TKI therapy in this cohort of patients with ALK-positive NSCLC, particularly those treated with alectinib. The numerical OS difference and imbalance in grade&#x2009;&#x2265;&#x2009;3 AEs should be interpreted cautiously because of the retrospective design and limited number of OS events. Further prospective studies with larger cohorts are warranted to validate these observations.
