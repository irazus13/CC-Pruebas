## Resumen

**INTRODUCTION/OBJECTIVES:** Clinical documentation is essential in dental practice but contributes to administrative workload, cognitive burden, and workflow inefficiencies. Ambient artificial intelligence (AI) has emerged as an approach that passively captures clinician-patient interactions and generates structured clinical records. This review aims to examine the concept of ambient AI and evaluate its potential applications, benefits, and challenges in dental clinical documentation and workflow optimization across different dental specialties. **DATA:** The available evidence consisted primarily of studies on ambient AI, AI-assisted documentation, and speech recognition from broader healthcare settings, together with a limited number of dental studies evaluating AI-assisted speech recognition. No studies specifically evaluated fully ambient AI documentation systems in routine dental practice. **SOURCES:** Peer-reviewed literature was identified through PubMed, Web of Science, and Google Scholar. Publicly available developer documentation was considered only to summarize currently available commercial platforms and was not used as evidence of clinical effectiveness. **STUDY SELECTION:** A narrative approach was used to review literature addressing AI-supported documentation systems, speech recognition technologies, and ambient AI platforms. As dental-specific evidence remains limited, relevant studies from broader healthcare settings were also included to provide contextual understanding of technological development, applications, current research gaps, and potential translation into dental workflows. **CONCLUSIONS:** Ambient AI represents a promising approach to improving clinical documentation in dentistry. However, no published studies have specifically evaluated fully ambient AI documentation systems in routine dental practice, and most anticipated benefits are extrapolated from broader healthcare literature rather than direct dental evidence. Well-designed dental-specific clinical studies are required before widespread clinical implementation can be recommended. **CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE:** Ambient AI may improve clinical documentation and workflow efficiency in dentistry. However, its clinical adoption should be guided by dental-specific evidence, which remains limited, and requires further clinical validation.
