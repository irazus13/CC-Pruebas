## Resumen

**OBJECTIVE:** To development and validate an artificial intelligence (AI) -based system for automatic full dentition labiolingual reconstruction (DenRec-LL) using Cone beam computed tomography (CBCT) images. **METHODS:** DenRec-LL was developed by generating 2D panoramic images, segmenting and identifying teeth with a transformer framework, mapping tooth centroids into 3D coordinates for 2D-3D alignment, performing 3D tooth segmentation using deep learning, and reconstructing the full dentition labiolingually based on estimated tooth axes, centers, and missing-tooth prediction. 75 external CBCT datasets (25 normal dentition, 25 mixed dentition and 25 crowded dentition) were collected and used for testing. The performance of DenRec-LL system was evaluated on a 4-point scale, the geometric deviation metrics were analyzed and the clinical acceptability rate (CAR) was calculated, and the reconstruction time was recorded. **RESULTS:** 75 CBCT datasets with 1500 teeth demonstrates high precision and stability across normal, mixed, and crowded dentition. A clear majority of labiolingual reconstruction cases (93.1%) achieved the highest score, and CAR was approximately 94% across groups, with no significant differences. The automated process required 63&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;4&#xa0;s per case, representing an approximately 4.85-fold reduction compared to the manual process. **CONCLUSIONS:** The DenRec-LL system could be used for full dentition reconstruction with high accuracy, robustness and efficiency. **CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE:** DenRec-LL bridges the gap between raw CBCT data and clinical decision&#x2011;making in routine orthodontic practice by automatically generating full&#x2011;dentition labiolingual images, which facilitate the assessment of root&#x2011;bone relationships and torque control.
