## Resumen

Implementation Science studies evidence practice gap separating innovation from clinical practice and designs interventions to close this gap. Process, determinant and evaluation frameworks provide a systematic way to ensure Implementation Science efforts are planned in a comprehensive and standardized format maintaining core components of the intervention but allowing for context-specific adaptations necessary for sustainment.
