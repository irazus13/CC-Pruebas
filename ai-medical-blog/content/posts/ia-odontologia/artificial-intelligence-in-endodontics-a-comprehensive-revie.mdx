## Resumen

The transformative impact of artificial intelligence (AI) on endodontics is gaining momentum. It highlights advancements in diagnostic accuracy, treatment planning and patient management. By integrating machine learning algorithms and imaging techniques, AI enhances clinical decision-making, reduces procedural errors and improves patient outcomes. Thus, data shows the current applications, challenges and future directions, proposing that AI will shape the future of endodontic practice and enhance the quality of dental care.
