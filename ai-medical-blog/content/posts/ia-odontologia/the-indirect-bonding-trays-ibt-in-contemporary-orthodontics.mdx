## Resumen

In recent years, the paradigm of orthodontic bracket placement has shifted decisively toward digital workflows. Indirect bonding tray (IBT) splints are facilitated by high-resolution intraoral scanning, artificial intelligence (AI), computer-aided design (CAD), and additive manufacturing (3D printing). This has become an alternative method to traditional direct bonding as the gold standard for precision. Additionally, the shift of orthodontic treatment methods to aligner therapy, force indirect bonding of the needed attachments. This paper reviews the most important information on IBT materials, with novel standards recently introduced due to the rapid development of their properties and manufacturing processes. The study summarizes the mechanical properties of new-generation biocompatible resins, the comparative accuracy of CAD/CAM transfer systems versus manual methods, and the economic impact of reduced chairside time on modern practice management. The indications and contraindications of using the IBT materials are also presented. The future perspectives of the use of these kinds of materials are highlighted. The scope of the study was to summarize the newest information on the procedures performed in orthodontic treatment.
