## Resumen

**PURPOSE:** This study applied uniform manifold approximation and projection (UMAP), a nonlinear dimensionality reduction algorithm, to high-dimensional data from immediately placed and immediately loaded dental implants in the anterior maxilla to identify distinct phenotypic clusters and explore whether preoperative characteristics are associated with 5-year esthetic outcomes in this exploratory proof-of-concept study. **MATERIALS AND METHODS:** Twenty-two patients who received immediately placed and immediately loaded implants in the anterior maxilla were analyzed. A total of 103 clinical, radiographic, and patient-reported parameters were collected across preoperative, baseline, 1-year, and 5-year time points. UMAP and principal component analysis (PCA) were used to generate two-dimensional representations of the data. K-means clustering was applied to the UMAP embeddings, and Mann-Whitney U tests with Bonferroni correction were used for inter-cluster comparisons. **RESULTS:** UMAP fitted on all 103 parameters separated the 22 implants into four clearly defined clusters. Clusters differed significantly in 5-year white esthetic score (WES; P &lt; .001 after Bonferroni correction), as well as in pink esthetic score (PES), orofacial esthetic scale (OES), soft tissue height, and buccal bone thickness (all P &lt; .05). PCA did not generate separable clusters. UMAP fitted on 24 preoperative parameters alone separated implants into three clusters that differed significantly in tooth location (P &lt; .001) and showed divergent 5-year outcomes in OES and periodontal probing depth (PPD). **CONCLUSIONS:** UMAP identified clinically meaningful phenotypic clusters among immediately placed and immediately loaded implants that were not apparent using conventional linear methods. Clusters characterized by favorable initial bone conditions achieved superior long-term esthetic outcomes. Preoperative UMAP clustering was associated with 5-year outcomes in this exploratory proof-of-concept analysis; validation in independent cohorts is needed.
