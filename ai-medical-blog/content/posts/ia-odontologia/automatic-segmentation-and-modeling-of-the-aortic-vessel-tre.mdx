## Resumen

The automated analysis of the aortic vessel tree (AVT) from computed tomography angiography (CTA) is crucial for clinical applications but lacks shared, high-quality data. To address this, we launched the SEG.A. challenge, introducing a large, public, multi-institutional dataset for AVT segmentation and benchmarking automated algorithms. The challenge results showed a strong trend toward deep learning, with 3D U-Net architectures being most effective. The winning solution used an ensemble-based strategy, highlighting the value of model ensembling for robust AVT segmentation. Performance strongly correlated with algorithmic design, notably the use of customized post-processing and training data characteristics. This initiative establishes a new performance benchmark and provides a lasting resource to drive future innovation toward robust, clinically translatable AVT analysis tools.
