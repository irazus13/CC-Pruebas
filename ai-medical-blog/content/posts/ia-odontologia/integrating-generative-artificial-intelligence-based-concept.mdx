## Resumen

Entrustable professional activities are important for training nursing students and enhancing their abilities to manage and care for pregnant women through clinical case studies. This study explored a generative artificial intelligence (AI)-based concept mapping (CM) approach within this framework to improve nursing students' learning outcomes in obstetrics education, focusing on critical thinking, cognitive load, and self-efficacy. Using a quasi-experimental design with pre-test and post-test evaluations, this study compared the effects of an AI-based CM activity with a conventional CM method. Results showed the AI-based approach significantly reduced cognitive load and improved students' critical thinking and self-efficacy. This innovative method demonstrated potential as an effective teaching strategy in nursing education. Nursing educators could incorporate such technologies to better support students' development in entrustable professional activities.
