## Resumen

Artificial intelligence (AI) is moving quickly from an emerging technology to a working component of dental practice. The pressing question for clinicians is no longer whether AI will influence dentistry but how to integrate it safely, ethically, and responsibly. Building on the authors' previous article on AI and the evolving standard of care, this article, the second in a series, addresses the standard of practice-the behaviors, safeguards, and professional responsibilities-that should guide dentists who use AI-assisted technologies. The authors propose a 10-domain framework: technology selection and understanding, independent verification, human oversight, privacy and data security, patient communication, documentation, bias awareness, separation of detection from treatment, performance monitoring, and professional accountability. An eight-stage AI clinical integration cycle (select &#x2192; understand &#x2192; apply &#x2192; verify &#x2192; decide &#x2192; communicate &#x2192; document &#x2192; monitor) translates these domains into daily workflow. Throughout, AI should serve as clinical decision support, not as an autonomous decision-maker; the dentist remains responsible for diagnosis, treatment recommendations, informed consent, and patient care.
