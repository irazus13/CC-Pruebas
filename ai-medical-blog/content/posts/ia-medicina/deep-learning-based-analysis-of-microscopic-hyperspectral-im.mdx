## Resumen

HER2 assessment in breast cancer relies on immunohistochemistry (IHC) with reflex in&#xa0;situ hybridization for equivocal cases, while conventional bright-field imaging provides limited quantitative optical information. We investigated microscopic hyperspectral imaging (HSI) of HER2-stained sections for slide-level molecular status prediction. HSI data from 43 cases (21 HER2-positive, 22 HER2-negative; 400-1000&#x2009;nm, 128 bands) were acquired between January 2024 and August 2025. After calibration, optical-density conversion, tissue masking, and Savitzky-Golay smoothing, we developed SpectralMix-MIL, a weakly supervised multiple-instance learning model combining learnable spectral projection (K&#x2009;=&#x2009;32) with attention pooling. On a patient-level 7:3 train/test split, SpectralMix-MIL achieved an AUROC of 0.923 and AUPRC of 0.910. Repeated stratified fivefold cross-validation yielded mean AUROC 0.904&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;0.041 and AUPRC 0.892&#x2009;&#xb1;&#x2009;0.045, while leave-one-out cross-validation yielded AUROC 0.897. These findings support microscopic HSI for quantitative breast cancer molecular phenotyping, pending larger external validation.
