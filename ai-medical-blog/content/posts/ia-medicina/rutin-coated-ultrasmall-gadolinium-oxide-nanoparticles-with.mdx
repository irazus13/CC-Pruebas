## Resumen

The straightforward one-step wet-chemical approach represents a promising strategy for the preparation of ultrasmall gadolinium oxide nanoparticles (GdONs) featuring high T1 relaxivity and excellent aqueous dispersion stability. However, achieving specificity for conventional GdONs still requires multi-step modifications with targeting molecules, introducing uncertainty into their application prospects. Herein, rutin (Ru)-coated ultrasmall GdONs (Gd@Ru) possessing both high T1 relaxivity and glucose transporter (GLUT)-overexpressing tumor-targeted magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) capability were successfully prepared via a one-step wet-chemical method. Among all the as-prepared Gd@Ru, Gd@Ru-5 with the lowest Gd content (&#x223c;3.8 wt% Gd, as determined by EDS) exhibits the smallest crystalline size (&#x223c; 1.8&#xa0;nm) and hydrodynamic diameter (&#x223c; 4.0&#xa0;nm), achieving the highest T1 relaxivity (40.4 mM- 1s- 1 at 1.5 T) along with exceptional stability. Moreover, Gd@Ru-5 showed satisfactory biosafety and excellent magnetic resonance angiography (MRA) performance in SD rats and New Zealand rabbits, as well as effective targeted imaging of GLUT-overexpressing 4T1 breast tumors. Overall, we present a facile one-step wet-chemical strategy for fabricating ultrasmall GdONs that integrate high T1 relaxivity, excellent MRA performance, and tumor-targeted MRI capability.
