## Resumen

The complex structures and dynamic processes of lipids make it challenging to decipher lipid reprogramming in metabolic diseases. Integrating artificial intelligence (AI) with lipidomics, mass spectrometry imaging, and lipid metabolic fluxomics enables rapid, high-precision, and in-depth spatiotemporal profiling of lipid species. In this review, we summarize advances in AI-empowered lipidomics, which not only accelerate spatiotemporal data processing but also offer new avenues to resolve the heterogeneity of lipid metabolism. Importantly, AI-assisted lipidomics could identify candidate biomarkers and generate testable hypotheses about disease mechanisms. Furthermore, we evaluate whether computational advances yield trustworthy evidence by examining annotation confidence, quantitative accuracy, cross-platform reproducibility, uncertainty calibration, and external validation. We also address the current limitations of AI-assisted lipidomics, particularly the risk of AI hallucinations.
