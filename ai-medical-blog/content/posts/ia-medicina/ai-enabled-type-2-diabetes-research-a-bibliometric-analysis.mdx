## Resumen

Artificial intelligence (AI) has been increasingly applied to type 2 diabetes (T2D) for risk prediction, complication assessment, and personalized management. However, the global development patterns and thematic evolution of AI-enabled T2D research remain unclear. This paper conducted a bibliometric analysis of 611 publications retrieved from the Web of Science Core Collection spanning the period from 2000 to 2025, using Bibliometrix and VOSviewer. Publication trends, geographical contributions, citation clustering, and thematic evolution were systematically analyzed. Results showed that AI-enabled T2D research experienced three developmental stages, with rapid growth after 2018. China and the United States were the leading contributors, accounting for over 60&#x202f;% of publications. Highly cited publications indicated that risk prediction has become a major research focus. Co-citation analysis revealed a progressive knowledge structure linking practical needs, methodological foundations, clinical evidence, and technological implementation. Thematic evolution demonstrated a shift from prediction-oriented applications toward deep learning, heterogeneous clinical data, biomarkers, explainable AI, and clinically oriented risk assessment. This review summarizes AI-enabled T2D research and analyzes future directions, providing references for clinical practice.
