## Resumen

**INTRODUCTION:** This study explored pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic factors associated with circulating vascular endothelial growth factor (VEGF) dynamics during bevacizumab treatment. **METHODS:** A secondary analysis was conducted using data from 74 patients with breast, ovarian, cervical, non-small cell lung, or colorectal cancer. Paired pre- and post-infusion serum concentrations of bevacizumab and VEGF were measured by ELISA. Exploratory dimensionality-reduction methods and supervised tree-based models were applied. Artificial neural networks were used only as a complementary exploratory analysis on the same dataset. **RESULTS:** The main outcome of the analysis was VEGF_D, the absolute difference between the paired VEGF concentrations. The supervised models did not perform above the naive three-class benchmark; therefore, their variable-importance outputs were treated as secondary, model-specific, and hypothesis-generating observations. The results show exploratory model patterns of bevacizumab exposure and circulating VEGF dynamics. We did not assess clinical efficacy, safety results, and other dosing strategies in this study. **DISCUSSION:** The results do not support clinical implementation, personalized dosing, or treatment optimization and need to be confirmed in large independent cohorts.
