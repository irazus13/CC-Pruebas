## Resumen

Transitions of care from hospital to outpatient settings represent a vulnerable period for patients with cancer, who often face complex treatment regimens, multiple comorbidities, and fragmented coordination across care settings. These challenges contribute to communication breakdowns and unplanned hospital readmissions. Advances in artificial intelligence (AI) and digital health technologies offer new opportunities to improve the transitional phases before, during, and after hospital discharge, and strengthen care continuity. This review examines emerging approaches enabled by AI and digital health across the inpatient-to-outpatient care spectrum for hospitalized patients with cancer. We discuss how predictive analytics and clinical decision support can identify patients at elevated risk of complications and guide discharge planning before hospitalization ends. We also review existing AI and digital health solutions tailored to the discharge process, including tools that enhance patient and caregiver education, medication reconciliation, follow-up coordination, discharge documentation, and management of pending test results. Finally, we explore post-discharge technologies, such as remote monitoring, symptom tracking systems, and AI-assisted communication platforms, that empower earlier detection of clinical deterioration and facilitate integration with outpatient and community-based resources. Overall, we highlight, as well as describe the limitations of, innovations that seek to improve coordination, reduce administrative burden, and support safer, more proactive transitions of care for patients undergoing cancer treatment.
