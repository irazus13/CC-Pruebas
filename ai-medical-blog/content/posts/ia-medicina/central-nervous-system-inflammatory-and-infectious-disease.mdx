## Resumen

PET enables noninvasive molecular characterization of central nervous system infectious and inflammatory diseases beyond conventional MR imaging capabilities. Translocator protein-PET quantifies microglial activation across multiple sclerosis, autoimmune encephalitis, and human immunodeficiency virus-associated neurocognitive disorders. Multitracer approaches differentiate neuromyelitis optica spectrum disorder from myelin oligodendrocyte glycoprotein antibody disease through distinct metabolic and inflammatory signatures, and pathogen-specific tracers address the critical limitation of distinguishing infection from sterile inflammation. Despite demonstrated diagnostic and prognostic value, clinical translation remains constrained by infrastructure requirements, genetic polymorphism effects, methodological variability, and lack of regulatory approval, necessitating multicenter harmonization and prospective validation studies.
