## Resumen

Purpose To compare the diagnostic accuracy of artificial intelligence (AI)-integrated and standard mammography screening pathways for breast cancer detection using Bayesian network meta-analysis. Materials and Methods This prospectively registered review searched PubMed, Embase, and Web of Science through March 20, 2026, for diagnostic accuracy studies comparing two or more human-only or AI-integrated screening pathways using digital mammography or tomosynthesis. Bayesian bivariate random-effects network meta-analyses were fitted to a primary four-node and a secondary six-node network; risk of bias was assessed using the Quality Assessment of Diagnostic Accuracy Studies 3 (QUADAS-3) and QUADAS-C tools, and certainty was graded using the Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation approach. Results Thirty-eight studies, with more than 4.2 million examinations, were included. In the primary digital mammography network (15 studies), AI as an independent reader (sensitivity, 78.9%; specificity, 95.8%) performed similarly to double human reading (sensitivity, 77.7%; specificity, 96.2%). In the secondary network, AI reader assistance had the highest sensitivity (86.8%) and AI triage had the highest specificity (96.4%). Per 10&#x2009;000 screened (prevalence, 0.8%), AI reader assistance detected nine more cancers than single human reading (69 vs 60), with 77 additional false-positive recalls (558 vs 481); AI triage had 27% fewer false-positive recalls than single human reading (353 vs 481), with the same cancer detection as double human reading (64 vs 64). Stand-alone AI had the lowest specificity (93.0%) and the most false-positive recalls (n = 694). Rankings are hypothesis-generating: Top-ranked pathways had overlapping credible intervals. Conclusion In this synthesis, AI reader assistance was associated with the highest cancer detection and AI triage with the lowest false-positive recall; stand-alone AI was not supported as a primary screening strategy in settings with existing radiologist capacity.
