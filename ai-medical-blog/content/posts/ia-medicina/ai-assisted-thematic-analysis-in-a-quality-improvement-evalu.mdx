## Resumen

Using artificial intelligence to identify themes in interview data from a quality improvement program evaluation produced 4 replicable themes grounded in the data. However, 2 consistently identified themes resulted from subtle misrepresentations and could have easily misled results without thorough data knowledge and output audit by the human research team.
