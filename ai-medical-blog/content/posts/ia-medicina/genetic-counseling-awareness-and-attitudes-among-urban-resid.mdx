## Resumen

In India, genetic counseling is an emerging service whose details are not well known. The present study evaluated the level of awareness about genetics and genetic counseling in the opinions of urban citizens in Bengaluru, a large Indian metropolis. In the cross-sectional study, a structured questionnaire was distributed among 200 residents across 8 administrative zones of Bengaluru, administered in-person and digitally through Google Forms. Attitudes toward genetic counseling, demographic information, and genetic awareness were measured. The relations between demographic and awareness levels were sought with the help of chi-square and Cramer's V analyses. Although 84% of the respondents were familiar with the term genetics and 78% familiar with genetic disorders, only 42.5% of the respondents were aware of genetic counseling and only 32.5% of respondents were aware of services in Bengaluru. Awareness was significantly associated with age and gender. 82.1% of the 18-24 age group had the highest awareness. Females (51.3%) were more aware than males (30.1%). Although there was limited knowledge of the availability of genetic counselor services, 78% of those who responded said they would make use of these services if they were available. These results clearly show that there is a huge gap in the knowledge base as while the general knowledge of genetic literacy is high, the knowledge of genetic counseling services is low in urban Bengaluru and varies from one group to another. Such results imply the necessity of specific public health campaigns, campaigns aimed at raising awareness, and the introduction of genetic counseling into the primary healthcare. This study found that while most people in Bengaluru are familiar with basic genetics, very few know that genetic counseling services exist or are available in the city. However, most people said they would be willing to use these services if they were made available. This suggests that raising awareness about where and how to access genetic counseling could quickly increase its use among the public.
