## Resumen

**BACKGROUND:** More than 90% of children with familial hypercholesterolemia (FH) remain undiagnosed. Although genetic testing is the diagnostic gold standard, limited availability necessitates the use of clinical diagnostic criteria, which have suboptimal performance in children. **OBJECTIVE:** To introduce the recently developed familial hypercholesterolemia pediatric diagnostic score (FH-PeDS) and its online implementation. **METHODS:** FH-PeDS was developed using real-world data from Slovenian children identified through universal cholesterol screening and externally validated in an independent Portuguese pediatric cohort. **RESULTS:** FH-PeDS demonstrated improved diagnostic accuracy compared with traditional clinical scoring systems and was recently recognized as promising tool in the European Atherosclerosis Society consensus statement on FH in children and adolescents. It is now freely available at https://fh-peds.eu in two versions: a semi-quantitative clinical scoring system and a machine learning model. **CONCLUSION:** The online FH-PeDS provides healthcare professionals with an accessible tool to estimate the likelihood of pediatric FH, potentially facilitating earlier diagnosis and treatment. Further external validation is warranted before widespread implementation.
