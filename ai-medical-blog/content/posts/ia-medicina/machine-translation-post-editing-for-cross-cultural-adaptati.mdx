## Resumen

**AIM:** To examine the feasibility of integrating machine translation post-editing (MTPE) into the cross-cultural adaptation of the Hospital Survey on Patient Safety Culture version 2.0 (HSOPSC 2.0) and to conduct a pilot psychometric evaluation of the resulting Traditional Chinese version (TC-HSOPSC 2.0). **DESIGN:** A two-phase methodological validation study. **METHODS:** From May to July 2025, HSOPSC 2.0 was translated and culturally adapted into Traditional Chinese using an MTPE workflow incorporating ChatGPT-4, Google Translate, BLEU-based screening, expert review, and iterative post-editing. Face validity was evaluated through cognitive interviews with 12 clinical nurses, followed by pilot psychometric testing with 32 clinical nurses. Reliability was assessed using Cronbach's alpha, ICC, and weighted kappa. **RESULTS:** Initial back-translation screening indicated that 68.3% of translated items exceeded the predefined BLEU criteria. Following expert review and iterative revision, this proportion increased to 70.7% for Google Translate and 80.5% for ChatGPT-4. Face validity assessment identified five items requiring revision, and item-level face validity indices improved from 0.58-0.67 to 0.92-1.00 after cognitive interviewing and expert review. The final TC-HSOPSC 2.0 demonstrated excellent internal consistency (Cronbach's &#x3b1;&#xa0;=&#xa0;0.93) and good test-retest reliability (ICC&#xa0;=&#xa0;0.74), with item-level weighted kappa coefficients ranging from 0.21 to 0.74, indicating fair-to-substantial agreement. **CONCLUSIONS:** The Nursing Education-Based Patient Safety Culture Assessment Tool was successfully translated and culturally adapted using an MTPE approach. The findings demonstrate that combining AI-assisted translation with expert review and cognitive interviewing can ensure translation quality and cultural relevance. This MTPE workflow provides a feasible and rigorous strategy for adapting nursing assessment instruments across diverse linguistic and cultural contexts. **IMPACT:** The MTPE workflow may facilitate the efficient adaptation of nursing and healthcare measurement instruments, thereby supporting the implementation and evaluation of evidence-based educational initiatives across diverse linguistic and cultural contexts.
