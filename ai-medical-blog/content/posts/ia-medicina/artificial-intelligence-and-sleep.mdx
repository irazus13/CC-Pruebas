## Resumen

This article explores how generative artificial intelligence (AI) and large language models are impacting the practice of sleep medicine from data analysis to patient interaction. The various AI advances in the field should be recognized as tools that have the potential to enhance care through increasing diagnostic expertise and reducing administrative burden. However, application in clinical settings require clinician familiarity and oversight to mitigate risks. As AI usage has become so ubiquitous and accessible, clinicians must navigate the blurring lines between patient-generated and clinical data, while preparing for a future where usage of AI may become a medicolegal necessity.
