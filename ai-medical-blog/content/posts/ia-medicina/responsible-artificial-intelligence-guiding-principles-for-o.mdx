## Resumen

AI is becoming deeply embedded in health care, driving demand for greater trust, governance, and responsible use. Responsible AI describes the design, development, deployment, and use of AI systems ethically, fairly, transparently, and accountably, ensuring societal benefit while mitigating risks, errors, and patient harm. These elements are critical to protecting patient safety and preserving trust in this technology. Orthopaedic surgeons should recognize AI's potential to improve musculoskeletal care quality, efficiency, and outcomes while upholding these fundamentals. Endorsing these tenets commits our field to advancing AI aligned with the highest ethical, clinical, and operational standards. We outline six guiding principles supporting safe, equitable, responsible AI adoption: (1) transparency, explainability, and trust, meaning safeguarding stakeholder trust in model development and clinical use; (2) clinical reliability and value orientation, meaning reliable, value-oriented models built on representative data and robust processing; (3) alignment with human values, meaning engaging patient and end-user needs across the development life cycle; (4) algorithmic fairness, meaning defining fairness and balancing tradeoffs; (5) accountability in equitable care delivery, meaning ensuring that models drive equitable care; and (6) clinician specialist oversight, meaning keeping clinicians involved in tool development. Adopting these principles enhances value and responsible use of this technology in orthopaedic care.
