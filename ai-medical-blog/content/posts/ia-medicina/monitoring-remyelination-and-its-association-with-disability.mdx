## Resumen

**BACKGROUND:** Conventional MRI has limited specificity for myelin quantification. IterAtive magnetic suscePtibility sources sepARaTion quantitative susceptibility mapping (APART-QSM) quantifies myelin-sensitive (&#x3c7;dia) and iron-sensitive (&#x3c7;para) susceptibility components, but its value for longitudinal assessment of remyelination in multiple sclerosis (MS) remains unclear. **PURPOSE:** To characterize longitudinal &#x3c7;dia in MS and assess its associations with clinical characteristics. **STUDY TYPE:** Prospective, longitudinal. **POPULATION:** One hundred forty-five patients with MS and 65 healthy controls (HCs). **FIELD STRENGTH/SEQUENCE:** 3.0T; 3D T1-weighted, 3D FLAIR, 3D DIR, and multi-echo gradient-echo imaging sequences. **ASSESSMENT:** APART-QSM generated &#x3c7;dia and &#x3c7;para maps, with susceptibility quantified in lesions and non-lesional regions, and the Expanded Disability Status Scale (EDSS) assessed at baseline and follow-up. **STATISTICAL TESTS:** Chi-square, Mann-Whitney U, Wilcoxon signed-rank, Friedman tests, linear mixed-effects models, linear and nonlinear regression. A p value &lt;&#x2009;0.05 was considered significant. **RESULTS:** One hundred one patients with MS and 20 HCs completed follow-up after a median (IQR) interval of 1.0 (0.8) and 1.25 (0.2) years, respectively. At baseline, white matter lesions (WMLs) exhibited lower &#x3c7;dia (0.0131 [IQR 0.0087] ppm) and higher &#x3c7;para (0.0058 [IQR 0.0049] ppm) than corresponding normal-appearing white matter (NAWM) (0.0191 [0.0088] ppm; 0.0036 [0.0038] ppm; both p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.001). Over 1&#x2009;year, &#x3c7;dia in WMLs increased (0.0122 [IQR 0.0069] vs. 0.0152 [IQR 0.0065] ppm, p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.001), particularly in ofatumumab-treated patients (0.0114 [IQR 0.0085] vs. 0.0158 [IQR 0.0081] ppm, p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.001). Greater increases in &#x3c7;dia were significantly associated with female sex, ofatumumab treatment, relapsing-remitting phenotype, and EDSS improvement (all Bonferroni correction p&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;0.01). &#x394;&#x3c7;dia showed a nonlinear decline with increasing disease duration (effective degrees of freedom (edf)&#x2009;=&#x2009;1.83, p&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.002). **CONCLUSION:** APART-QSM may enable longitudinal monitoring of remyelination-related changes in MS, with declining &#x394;&#x3c7;dia over disease duration potentially informing individualized treatment strategies. **STAGE OF TECHNICAL EFFICACY:** 2.
