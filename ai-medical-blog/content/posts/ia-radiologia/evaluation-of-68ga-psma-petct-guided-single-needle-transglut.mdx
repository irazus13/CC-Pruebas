## Resumen

Objective The aim of this study is to evaluate the performance of 68Ga-PSMA PET/CT-guided transgluteal single-needle targeted biopsy (PSMA-TB) for detecting prostate cancer (PCa) and clinically significant prostate cancer (csPCa), in comparison with transrectal ultrasound-guided biopsy (TRUS-GB).MethodsA retrospective analysis was conducted on 460 cases of suspected prostate cancer admitted to Nanjing First Hospital between January 2018 and June 2024. According to the biopsy method, the patients were divided into a TRUS-GB group (n = 324) and a PSMA-TB group (n = 136). Clinical, imaging, biopsy pathological, and postoperative pathological data after prostatectomy were collected for statistical analysis.ResultsThere was no significant difference in age and SUVmax between the two groups (P &gt; 0.05). PSA levels and PI-RADS scores were significantly higher in the PSMA-TB group than those of the TRUS-GB group (P&lt;0.05). The true positive rates of PSMA-TB for PCa and csPCa were higher than those of TRUS-GB (PCa: 76.2% vs 38.0%, P&lt;0.01; csPCa: 71.3% vs 28.4%, P&lt;0.01). Within the PSMA-TB group, the true positive rates for PCa and csPCa were higher than the false negative rates (P&lt;0.01). The pathological consistency between PSMA-TB biopsy and postoperative prostatectomy specimens was higher than that of TRUS-GB (P = 0.004 1).ConclusionPSMA-TB demonstrates higher diagnostic accuracy for both PCa and csPCa compared with TRUS-GB. Characterized by its minimal invasiveness and higher consistency with postoperative radical prostatectomy pathology, PSMA-TB represents an optimal approach for the precise diagnosis of suspected prostate lesions.
