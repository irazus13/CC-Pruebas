## Resumen

Artificial intelligence (AI) continues to revolutionize the healthcare industry, with Radiology experiencing substantial breakthroughs due to its inherently technical nature. With a concern for job displacement, AI is often perceived as a potential threat by radiologists. However, this paper outlines comprehensive ways through which radiologists with a proficiency in AI can be empowered to undertake critical responsibilities, positioning them as the "Radiologist 2.0" in the AI-driven modern era.
