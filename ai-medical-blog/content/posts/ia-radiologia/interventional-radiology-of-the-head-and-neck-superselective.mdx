## Resumen

Interventional radiology (IVR) of the head and neck is an essential component of multidisciplinary care, serving to intensify local treatment, to reduce the risk of perioperative bleeding, and to provide life-saving control of catastrophic hemorrhage. This review outlines endovascular treatment in three settings-superselective intra-arterial chemoradiotherapy (RADPLAT), preoperative tumor embolization, and emergency hemostasis for intractable epistaxis and carotid blowout syndrome (CBS)-with emphasis on clinical indications together with their advantages and limitations, anatomical considerations including dangerous anastomoses, devices and techniques, avoidance of complications, pre- and post-procedural imaging, and the application of artificial intelligence (AI).
