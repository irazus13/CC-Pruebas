## Resumen

Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly being integrated into veterinary diagnostic imaging, yet robust regulatory oversight, validation standards, and governance frameworks remain limited. This review examines the current landscape of veterinary radiology AI, emphasizing differences between AI-generated outputs and comprehensive radiologist interpretations. It highlights key sources of algorithmic and human cognitive bias, explains AI development and validation processes, and outlines common pitfalls in interpreting performance metrics. The article stresses that veterinarians retain responsibility for clinical decisions and should critically evaluate AI tools for transparency, intended use, validation, and clinical utility. Responsible implementation requires human oversight, documentation, education, and ongoing monitoring.
