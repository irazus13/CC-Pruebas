## Resumen

**BACKGROUND:** Accurate identification of carotid intraplaque hemorrhage (IPH), a key marker of plaque vulnerability, remains challenging on computed tomography angiography (CTA). We aimed to identify CTA-derived features associated with IPH and develop a CTA-based diagnostic model using high-resolution vessel wall imaging (HR-VWI) as the reference standard. **METHODS:** In this multicenter retrospective study, 469 patients with 1,196 carotid plaques who underwent CTA and HR-VWI within 1&#x202f;month were enrolled. IPH was defined on HR-VWI. Qualitative CTA features, including the rim sign, and quantitative plaque characteristics derived from artificial intelligence-assisted CTA analysis were assessed. Independent predictors of IPH were identified using multivariable logistic regression. A diagnostic model was developed in a training cohort, and validated internally and externally. **RESULTS:** IPH was present in 277 of 1,196 plaques (23.2&#x202f;%). The rim sign (OR, 13.7; 95&#x202f;% CI, 6.4-29.4), fibro-lipid volume (OR, 1.5; 95&#x202f;% CI, 1.2-2), and luminal stenosis (OR, 2.2; 95&#x202f;% CI, 1.6-2.9) were independently associated with IPH (all P&#x202f;&lt;&#x202f;0.001). Luminal stenosis and fibro-lipid volume showed the highest discriminative performance (AUCs, 0.741 and 0.735), while the rim sign demonstrated high specificity (97.9&#x202f;%). The combined CTA model achieved AUCs of 0.794, 0.829, and 0.803 in the training, internal validation, and external validation cohorts, respectively. **CONCLUSIONS:** The rim sign, fibro-lipid volume, and luminal stenosis are independent CTA predictors of carotid IPH. Integrating qualitative CTA markers with AI-derived quantitative plaque features enables accurate, noninvasive identification of IPH and may improve CTA-based assessment of carotid plaque vulnerability.
