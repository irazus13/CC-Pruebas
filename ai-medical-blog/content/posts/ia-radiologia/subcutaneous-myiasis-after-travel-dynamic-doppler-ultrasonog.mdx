## Resumen

**PURPOSE:** To describe dynamic ultrasonographic findings in travel-associated subcutaneous myiasis. **METHODS:** A 54-year-old man with persistent nodular lesions after rural travel underwent gray-scale and color Doppler ultrasonography using a 13-MHz high-frequency linear transducer. **RESULTS:** Imaging demonstrated an echogenic subcutaneous structure with posterior acoustic shadowing, surrounding hyperemia, and real-time rhythmic internal movement, supporting larval infestation. **CONCLUSION:** Recognition of internal larval motion on ultrasonography may help distinguish myiasis from abscess, foreign body or vascularized soft-tissue masses and guide definitive mechanical extraction.
