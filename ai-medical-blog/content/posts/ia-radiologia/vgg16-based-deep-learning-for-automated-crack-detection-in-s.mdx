## Resumen

Detection and classification of faults in solar panels are important for maintaining photovoltaic system performance and identifying damaged modules at an early stage. This study proposes a VGG16-based deep learning framework, termed Crack Deep Learning Algorithm (CD-DLA), for automated classification of solar-panel images. The framework extracts discriminative visual features to identify different solar-panel conditions. The study used a Kaggle dataset containing 875 images distributed across six classes: Bird-drop, Clean, Dusty, Electrical-damage, Physical-Damage, and Snow-Covered. The dataset was divided into 70% training, 15% validation, and 15% testing subsets. On the independent held-out test set, the proposed VGG16 model achieved an accuracy of 98.47%. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of the proposed framework for the evaluated solar-panel condition classification task. However, because the evaluation was performed on a single dataset, further testing on independent datasets is required to assess generalization across different solar-panel types and imaging conditions.
