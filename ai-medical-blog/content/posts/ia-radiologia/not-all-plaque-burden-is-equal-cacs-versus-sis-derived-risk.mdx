## Resumen

**PURPOSE:** To assess the prognostic value of plaque burden based on CACS and SIS categories over CAD-RADS. **METHODS:** In this retrospective, single-center study, 2850 adults who underwent elective CCTA between 2016 and 2020 were screened. A total of 1818 patients were enrolled based on very low-to-intermediate pre-test risk, good image quality (Likert score &#x2265;4), and &#x2265;3 years of follow-up. Clinical data were collected, and CAD-RADS 2.0 with CACS-based plaque burden (CACS-P score) and SIS-based plaque burden (SIS-P score) was quantified. Prediction of 5-point MACE composite outcome (late unplanned coronary revascularization, non-fatal myocardial infarction, hospitalization for heart failure, hospitalization for angina, all-cause mortality) and 2-point MACE composite outcome (non-fatal myocardial infarction and all-cause mortality) was evaluated using survival analysis and multivariable models. **RESULTS:** During a median available follow-up of 62 months, 178/1818 patients (9.8%) experienced a 5-point MACE and 96/1818 (5.3%) experienced myocardial infarction/death within the fixed 36-month analysis horizon. Both CAD-RADS severity and plaque burden scores were associated with adverse outcomes. Integration of the CACS-based P-score identified revealed heterogeneity in risk estimates within CAD-RADS categories. For the primary endpoint, CAD-RADS 3/P0 showed HR point estimates of similar magnitude to those observed in CAD-RADS 4-5/P0 and CAD-RADS 4-5/P3-P4 (HR 15.95 [95% CI:3.95-64.36], HR 16.21 [3.23-81.31], and HR 14.13 [5.70-35.01], respectively). For myocardial infarction/death, CAD-RADS 2-3/P0 showed HR point estimates of similar magnitude to those observed in CAD-RADS 4-5/P1-P2. Conversely, SIS-P provided limited additional within category risk stratification. **CONCLUSIONS:** CACS-based P score may refine risk stratification in patients with mild-to-moderate stenosis.
