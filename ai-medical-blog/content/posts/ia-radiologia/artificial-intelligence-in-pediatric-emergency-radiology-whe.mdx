## Resumen

Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly being explored in pediatric emergency radiology, where timely and accurate image interpretation is critical. Current evidence highlights its strongest role in detection and localization tasks across neuroimaging, thoracic, abdominopelvic, and musculoskeletal studies. AI also shows promise for classification, severity assessment, and workflow prioritization, improving diagnostic accuracy and efficiency in some settings. However, existing research is limited by retrospective designs, small datasets, insufficient external validation, and poor transferability of adult-trained models. Pediatric-specific models, multicenter validation, prospective studies, and thoughtful clinical integration are needed before wider adoption beyond assistive use in routine pediatric emergency imaging practice today.
