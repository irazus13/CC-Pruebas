## Resumen

Low back pain is a frequent reason for consultation with the general practitioner and in specialized settings. Apart from alarming signs, care must be taken not to obtain early radiological imaging and especially not to provide the patient with the radiological protocol without a minimum of explanations about the degenerative changes that occur physiologically, especially after 50 years.
