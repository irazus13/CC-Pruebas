## Resumen

**BACKGROUND:** We developed and validated a portal venous phase CT-based model that combines topological intratumoral heterogeneity (ITH), radiomics, and clinicoradiological features for preoperative prediction of microvascular invasion (MVI) in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC). We also examined whether three-dimensional (3D) ITH was associated with transcriptomic and immune-related features in an exploratory radiogenomic cohort. **METHODS:** This multicenter retrospective study included 821 surgically confirmed HCC patients in training (n&#x2009;=&#x2009;489), internal validation (n&#x2009;=&#x2009;211), and external validation (n&#x2009;=&#x2009;121) cohorts. Manually segmented tumors were used to develop clinical, ITH, radiomics, and stacking ensemble models. Time to recurrence (TTR) and SHAP-based model interpretation were analyzed; mortality data were not collected, and death was therefore not incorporated into this recurrence endpoint. A separate supplementary, hypothesis-generating analysis of 42 TCGA-LIHC cases explored transcriptomic correlates of 3D ITH; differential-expression and gene-set analyses used Benjamini-Hochberg false-discovery rate (FDR) correction. **RESULTS:** The ensemble achieved AUCs of 0.805 (95% CI, 0.760-0.847), 0.851 (95% CI, 0.796-0.902), and 0.757 (95% CI, 0.648-0.855) in the training out-of-fold, internal validation, and external validation cohorts, respectively. The ITH model showed comparable and numerically slightly higher AUCs than the radiomics model, but paired DeLong tests did not show statistically significant differences. SHAP analysis ranked the 3D ITH score as the leading contributor within the ensemble, and both the 3D ITH score and ensemble risk score were associated with TTR. The supplementary TCGA-LIHC analysis generated exploratory associations between 3D ITH and survival and pathway-level transcriptomic patterns. **CONCLUSIONS:** Portal venous CT-derived topological ITH provided interpretable information for preoperative MVI prediction and recurrence-risk assessment in HCC. The supplementary radiogenomic observations are hypothesis-generating.
