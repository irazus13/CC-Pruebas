## Resumen

Cardiovascular shape is an active participant in disease, coupling anatomy, mechanics, and hemodynamics in a self-reinforcing loop. Statistical shape modeling (SSM) makes this loop quantifiable, converting 3 dimensional imaging into compact, interpretable descriptors of cardiac form. This article organizes the cardiac SSM literature into 3 complementary strands: descriptive studies that link shape to outcome, mechanistic studies that couple geometry to physics-based simulation, and enabling applications spanning segmentation, atlases, virtual cohorts, and deep-learning subtyping. We highlight the shape-flow-function paradigm, summarize methodological principles, and outline the barriers, regulatory acceptance, and workflow integration.
