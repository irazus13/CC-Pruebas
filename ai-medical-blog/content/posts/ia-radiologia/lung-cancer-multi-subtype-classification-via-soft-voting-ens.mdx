## Resumen

Lung cancer is a highly heterogeneous malignant tumour, and accurate classification of its subtypes is relevant to personalized treatment planning. However, overlapping imaging appearances and severe class imbalance make multi-subtype classification challenging. To address this problem, we propose a soft-voting ensemble of EfficientNet-B3, ConvNeXt-Tiny, and DenseNet-121. The three independently optimized backbones were selected for their complementary architectural characteristics and the framework does not explicitly disentangle or fuse their feature representations. Instead, it averages their class-probability outputs at inference. Experiments on a public dataset containing 18,500 CT-PET images show that the ensemble achieves an accuracy of 0.9865 and an F1 score of 0.9865. The class-wise recalls for adenocarcinoma, large cell carcinoma, small cell carcinoma, and squamous cell carcinoma are 99.55%, 100.00%, 96.17%, and 98.20%, respectively. These retrospective image-level results support further investigation of the framework as a potential radiologist-assistance tool.
