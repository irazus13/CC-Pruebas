## Resumen

**PURPOSE:** To assess whether ChatGPT-4 improves readability of breast radiology reports by generating simplified summaries and patient-centered recommendations. **METHODS:** Seventy-five BI-RADS 3, 4, and 5 breast imaging reports were entered into ChatGPT-4 using two prompts: (1) "Please put the report into layperson terms" and (2) "Please provide recommendations for the patient." Readability of original reports and AI-generated outputs was assessed using validated readability metrics, including the Flesch Reading Ease score, Gunning Fog Index, Flesch-Kincaid Grade Level, Coleman-Liau Index, and SMOG Index. Two breast radiologists independently reviewed the AI-generated layperson summaries and recommendations for medical accuracy. Statistical analysis was performed using two-sided, unpaired, equal-variance t-tests. **RESULTS:** ChatGPT-4-generated layperson summaries demonstrated significantly improved readability compared with the original reports across BI-RADS 3, 4, and 5 categories (all p&#x202f;&lt;&#x202f;0.05). ChatGPT-4-generated patient recommendations also demonstrated significantly improved readability for BI-RADS 3 reports. For BI-RADS 4 reports, readability improved across several readability metrics with significance observed for a subset of metrics. In contrast, for BI-RADS 5 reports, ChatGPT-4-generated recommendations were less readable than the original reports on several readability metrics. Despite these differences in readability, all AI-generated summaries and recommendations were deemed medically accurate by two breast radiologists. **CONCLUSION:** ChatGPT-4 generates medically accurate, patient-friendly summaries of BI-RADS 3-5 breast imaging reports with improved readability and may serve as a useful tool for enhancing patient understanding of breast imaging results. However, ChatGPT-4-generated patient recommendations demonstrated variable readability with reduced readable for BI-RADS 5 reports, highlighting the need for further refinement before clinical implementation.
