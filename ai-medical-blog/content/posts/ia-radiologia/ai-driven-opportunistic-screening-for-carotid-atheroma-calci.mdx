## Resumen

To develop a labeled image dataset of suspected carotid artery calcifications (CACs) on dental panoramic radiographs (PRs) and to propose a deep learning-based framework for their automated detection and segmentation. A retrospective dataset comprising 19,205 PRs obtained from our institution (between 2015 and 2023) was analyzed. After manual labeling, 372 images showing CACs and 322 controls were included. A hybrid architecture combining FasTVit, AttentionNet, and DC-UNet was implemented for classification, detection, and segmentation. Model performance was assessed using accuracy, precision, recall, the Dice coefficient, intersection over union (IoU), and area under the curve (AUC). The study evaluated two scenarios: one in which detection and segmentation were performed after classification, and another in which the classification was omitted. The classification model achieved an accuracy of 0.8780, a precision of 0.8779, a recall of 0.8795, and a specificity of, 0.8795. Incorporating the classification step improved precision from 0.48 to 0.93, accuracy from 0.79 to 0.97, and specificity from 0.75 to 0.98. The final framework achieved an area under the ROC curve of 0.96. The proposed hybrid artificial intelligence (AI) framework showed promising internal performance for the opportunistic detection and localization of radiographic findings suggestive of CACs on PRs. Within the limitations of this study, the proposed framework may serve as a complementary aid to dentists by flagging suspected CACs during routine interpretation of PRs and facilitating timely referral for confirmatory cardiovascular assessment.
