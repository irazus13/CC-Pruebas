## Resumen

Purpose To evaluate the performance of Sybil, a Medical Open Network for Artificial Intelligence (MONAI)-based nodule model, and their combination for 1-year lung cancer detection in two screening cohorts: the National Lung Screening Trial (NLST) and the Pamplona International Early Lung Cancer Action Program (P-IELCAP). Materials and Methods Baseline low-dose CT (LDCT) examinations from the NLST and the P-IELCAP were analyzed. A prespecified OR-stacked composite model combining Sybil, a MONAI-based nodule model, and a safety-net rule (MONAI-detected nodule &gt; 6 mm with detection confidence &gt; 0.95) was developed and evaluated for 1-year lung cancer detection. Model discrimination was assessed by using area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AUC), and operating-point performance metrics were calculated. Results A total of 5110 baseline LDCT examinations were analyzed, including 2343 from NLST and 2767 from P-IELCAP, with 65 lung cancers diagnosed within 1 year. Mean age, mean pack-years, and percentage of female participants were 61.6 years &#xb1; 5.1 (SD), 57.3 pack-years &#xb1; 24.8, and 39.4% (923 of 2343), respectively, in NLST and 56.0 years &#xb1; 9.5, 34.4 pack-years &#xb1; 21.5, and 28.6% (791 of 2767), respectively, in P-IELCAP. Lung cancer incidence (per 1000 person-years) was higher in NLST than in P-IELCAP (17.7 vs 8.7; P = .006). Mean Sybil 1-year lung cancer probabilities (scaled by 1000 for presentation) were also higher in NLST than in P-IELCAP (16.4 vs 9.9; P &lt; .01). Sybil achieved an AUC of 0.93 in both cohorts, whereas the MONAI-based model achieved AUCs of 0.90 in NLST and 0.79 in P-IELCAP. The composite model missed two of 65&#x2009;1-year lung cancers while correctly classifying 2731 of 4965 (55.0%) noncancer examinations as negative, yielding a sensitivity of 63 of 65 (96.9%) and a positive predictive value of 63 of 2297 (2.7%). Conclusion For 1-year lung cancer detection with LDCT, the composite AI model achieved high sensitivity while maintaining acceptable specificity.
