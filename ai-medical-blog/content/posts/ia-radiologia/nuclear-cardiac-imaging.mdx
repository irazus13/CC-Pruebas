## Resumen

Nuclear cardiology is a broad topic but is commonly used for evaluation of inducible ischemia in patients with symptoms suggestive of coronary artery disease. Tc-99 m sestamibi and tetrofosmin single-photon emission computed tomography have long been mainstays in the evaluation of reversible perfusion defects indicative of ischemia; however, cardiac PET, including Rb-82 PET, has gained widespread use because it permits quantification of myocardial blood flow and myocardial blood flow reserve. Abnormal myocardial blood flow reserve may indicate epicardial coronary artery disease, diffuse atherosclerosis, and/or coronary microvascular dysfunction even when relative perfusion appears normal, and it can alter risk stratification.
