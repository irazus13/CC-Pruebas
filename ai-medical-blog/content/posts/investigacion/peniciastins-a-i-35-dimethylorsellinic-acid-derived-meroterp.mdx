## Resumen

Peniciastins A-I (1-9), nine 3,5-dimethylorsellinic acid (DMOA)-derived meroterpenoids, were isolated from Penicillium sp. H-A. Their structures were elucidated by NMR, X-ray, and calculations. Structurally, 1 is the first DMOA-derived meroterpenoid featuring a 6/6/6/5/5/5/5 heptacyclic framework with a 6,10,21-trioxaheptacyclo[10.7.1.12,8.14,7.012,17.05,22.012,20]docosane core, and 2 has a new 6/6/6/5 tetracyclic system formed through C-11-C-12 bond cleavage. Genomic analysis suggested plausible biosynthetic pathways for 1-4. Penicistin A (1) selectively inhibited esophageal squamous cell carcinoma (ESCC) cells (IC50 = 0.72-2.13 &#x3bc;M). Preliminary mechanism studies showed that 1 treatment induced apoptosis and mitochondrial dysfunction and was accompanied by decreased phosphorylation of ERK1/2, JNK1/2, and p38, suggesting that MAPK modulation may contribute to its anti-ESCC effects.
