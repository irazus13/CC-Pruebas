## Resumen

Artificial intelligence (AI) has rapidly expanded its applications in medicine, demonstrating potential in imaging analysis, risk prediction, and decision support. Owing to the complexity of disease patterns and the experience-dependent nature of clinical decision-making, AI holds considerable promise in cervical spine surgery. However, most existing studies remain focused on retrospective modeling and technical validation, with limited evidence supporting clinical usability and generalizability. From a clinically driven perspective, this review summarizes the major application scenarios and developmental trends of AI in cervical spine surgery, and highlights key challenges related to causal inference, data quality, multi-center collaboration, and legal and ethical considerations, aiming to provide a rational basis for the standardized application of AI in this field.
