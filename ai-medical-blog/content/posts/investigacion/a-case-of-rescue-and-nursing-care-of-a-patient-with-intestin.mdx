## Resumen

This article reports the treatment and nursing care of a patient with intestinal necrosis secondary to acute severe organophosphate pesticide poisoning. After accidentally ingesting approximately 100 mL of an undiluted dichlorvos formulation, the patient rapidly developed toxic shock. In the early stage, the recognition of intestinal necrosis was difficult due to overlapping symptoms. Through continuous monitoring of abdominal signs, blood lactate levels, and tissue perfusion status, the medical and nursing team identified the disease progression in a timely manner and, relying on multidisciplinary collaboration, carried out aggressive detoxification, organ function support, and surgical intervention for intestinal necrosis, together with continuous perioperative nursing care and psychological support. After 25 days of aggressive treatment and nursing care, the patient recovered and was discharged. A follow-up visit one month after discharge showed that the patient was in stable condition, with activities of daily living basically restored.
