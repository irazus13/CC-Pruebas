## Resumen

The management of aorto-iliac aneurysms has evolved considerably with advances in imaging, endovascular techniques, and perioperative care. Artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) have emerged as promising tools to enhance the management of vascular diseases including abdominal aortic aneurysm (AAA). This review provides an overview of recent developments in AI applied to aorto-iliac aneurysm management. By integrating clinical, imaging, and biomechanical data, AI-based approaches have shown promising results in predicting aneurysm growth and rupture, contributing to develop support for decision making. In addition, automated image analysis has enabled rapid and reproducible assessment of vascular anatomy, supporting preoperative planning and device sizing. Emerging technologies, including digital twins and extended reality, have further contributed to personalized procedural strategies and enhanced intraoperative guidance. In the postoperative setting, AI may also improve the detection and prediction of complications. Despite these advances, several challenges still have to be faced and ongoing collaborative efforts aiming at standardization, large-scale data integration, infrastructure, overcoming ethical and legal challenges, developing continuous learning models, multimodal AI prediction models and implementation and validation, remain essential for the use of AI for personalized therapy in aorto-iliac aneurysms.
