## Resumen

BAX is a critical regulator of the apoptosis cascade and its abnormal activation contributes to the development of disease including cardiovascular and neurodegenerative disorders. Therefore, BAX represents an attractive target for the pharmacological inhibition of pathological cell death. Based on a virtual screen, we identified and modified several EGFR kinase inhibitors with potential activity against BAX. Systematic modifications of the dacomitinib and gefitinib core structure were performed, allowing us to elucidate how structural modifications influence biological activity and leading to a comprehensive structure-activity relationship (SAR) on BAX inhibition. The developed series has favorable drug-like properties and improved BAX inhibitory activity in vitro, in cells, and in human induced pluripotent stem cell-derived brain organoids, with significantly reduced EGFR kinase activity. This series therefore represents a promising starting point for the development of improved and potentially therapeutic BAX inhibitors.
