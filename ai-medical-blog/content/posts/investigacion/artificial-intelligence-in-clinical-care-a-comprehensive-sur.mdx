## Resumen

BackgroundIn our research work an extensive survey we thoroughly explore how artificial intelligence (AI), particularly machine learning and deep learning techniques, are changing healthcare with the goal of improving disease detection, diagnosis, and treatment. The paradigm changes from healthcare 4.0 to healthcare 5.0 is being driven by AI.ObjectiveThis work begins with the roadmap of various enabling technologies that important in medical fields.MethodsThe work established by a methodical literature review and bibliometric approach to investigate different promising technologies like artificial intelligence, deep learning, natural language processing, and traditional machine learning, and assesses their applications in healthcare.ResultsImportant discoveries show that how artificial intelligent greatly increases diagnostic precision and supports personalized medicine; yet, incorporating these technologies into clinical processes, presents difficulties, including concerns about data quality, privacy, and ethics.ConclusionSeveral examples of healthcare use cases to improved interpretability and sophisticated AI models are needed in the future in healthcare 5.0, Lastly, combined with regulatory frameworks to guarantee deployment in healthcare settings both safe and efficient in future research.
