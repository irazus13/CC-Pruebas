## Resumen

**BACKGROUND:** Prolonged QRS duration portends worse outcomes in heart failure with reduced ejection fraction (HFrEF). In GALACTIC-HF, omecamtiv mecarbil (OM) reduced the primary composite endpoint of first heart failure (HF) event or cardiovascular death. **OBJECTIVES:** The objectives of this study were to evaluate whether QRS duration and the presence of cardiac resynchronization therapy (CRT) modify the treatment effect of OM. **METHODS:** GALACTIC-HF enrolled patients with symptomatic HF, HFrEF (&#x2264;35%), and elevated natriuretic peptides. Baseline QRS duration was available in 8,089 of 8,232 participants (98.0%), who were classified into 3 groups: QRS duration &lt;120 ms without CRT (n = 3,724), QRS duration &#x2265;120 ms without CRT (n = 3,225), and CRT regardless of QRS duration (n = 1,140). The authors assessed whether the effect of OM on the primary composite endpoint, first HF hospitalization, cardiovascular death, and all-cause death differed across these groups. **RESULTS:** Patients with QRS &#x2265;120 ms without CRT and those with CRT were older, had lower EF, worse health status, and higher N-terminal pro-B-type natriuretic peptide levels than those with QRS &lt;120 ms without CRT. When modeled as a continuous variable, baseline QRS duration was associated with clinical outcomes among patients without CRT but not among those with CRT. Among patients with QRS &#x2265;120 ms without CRT, OM appeared to confer less benefit for the primary composite endpoint (Pinteraction = 0.072), for cardiovascular death (Pinteraction = 0.003), and for all-cause death (Pinteraction = 0.009). In contrast, among patients treated with CRT, OM was beneficial regardless of QRS duration. **CONCLUSIONS:** In GALACTIC-HF, OM appeared less effective in patients with prolonged QRS duration who did not have CRT, whereas it remained beneficial in CRT-treated patients. (Global Approach to Lowering Adverse Cardiac Outcomes Through Improving Contractility in Heart Failure [GALACTIC-HF]; NCT02929329).
