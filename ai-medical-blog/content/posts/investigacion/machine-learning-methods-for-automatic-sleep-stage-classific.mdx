## Resumen

The intricacies of sleep architecture hold significant implications for understanding human health, making automated sleep staging a critical frontier in sleep research. While existing literature predominantly delves into deep learning methods for sleep staging, the current landscape of lightweight, home-use sleep monitoring devices necessitates a closer examination of traditional machine learning algorithms for sleep pattern recognition and analysis. In response to this need, in this paper we provide a comprehensive review of machine learning methods for automatic sleep staging. By synthesizing state-of-the-art approaches, we traverse the entire process, from data collection devices to feature extraction and selection, culminating in diverse sleep stage classification methods. These methods encompass single-modality, dual-modality, and multi-modality approaches, reflecting the evolving complexity of sleep stage classification methodologies. Moreover, we delve into the challenges, identify emerging trends, and outline future directions in the field of sleep staging. By offering these insights, we aim to equip researchers, clinicians, and technologists with a roadmap for advancing automated sleep staging methodologies within the realm of machine learning.
