## Resumen

Early weight-loss responses to oral glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 RAs) vary substantially, and super-responders are incompletely characterized. Using de-identified electronic health records, we studied 9287 GLP-1 RA-naive patients prescribed oral Wegovy. Of 8381 patients with an index weight, 3011 had a weight between 30 and 180 days and were classified by their best percent weight change as non-responders (no loss), responders (&gt;0%-5% loss), strong responders (&gt;5%-10%), or super-responders (&gt;10%). Body weight was traced back 5 years, and HbA1c, blood pressure, comorbidities, dose escalation, and phenotypes extracted from clinical notes using Artificial Intelligence (AI)-augmented curation were compared across these treatment response groups. Super-responders weighed less at initiation than non-responders (94.1 vs. 101.7 kg; P&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;.001) and had weighed 7.6% less 5 years earlier, against 0.8% less among non-responders (P&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;.001). Among patients of the same starting weight, faster weight gain between 5 years and 6 months before treatment predicted lower weight at day 90 (0.11 kg per year; P&#x2009;=&#x2009;.015). Super-responders lost 7.2% by day 60 and 11.4% by day 90, returning within 2 months to their weight from 5 years earlier. Baseline Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) and blood pressure did not differ across groups, and the share of women and of type 2 diabetes varied only modestly with response. Super-responders escalated earlier (37% vs. 24% were prescribed &gt;1.5 mg at day 30; P&#x2009;=&#x2009;.001) and their systolic blood pressure fell more by day 90 (10.2 vs. 3.3 mmHg; P&#x2009;=&#x2009;.006). Among 1978 phenotypes AI-curated from unstructured clinical notes, before the first prescription, severe obesity was documented less in super-responders (19.4% vs. 36.1%; P&#x2009;&lt;&#x2009;.001; q&#x2009;=&#x2009;0.003). Pre-treatment weight trajectory and dosage emerged as associates of early response, but the outcome-defined groups and short follow-up make these findings hypothesis-generating.
