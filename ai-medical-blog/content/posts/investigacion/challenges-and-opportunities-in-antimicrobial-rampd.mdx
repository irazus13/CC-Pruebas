## Resumen

Antimicrobial resistance is accelerating, while the discovery and development of new drugs, particularly those with novel mechanisms of action, have failed to keep pace. This widening gap reflects two major challenges: insufficient resources for antibiotic discovery and clinical development, and a lack of scientific innovation. With pharmaceutical companies largely abandoning antibiotic R&amp;D, academic groups, small biotech companies, and not-for-profits are stepping in, harnessing advances in genomics, synthetic biology, and AI to drive discovery. Coupled with growing international investment and initiatives to address funding gaps, these efforts offer renewed hope for overcoming the antimicrobial resistance crisis.
