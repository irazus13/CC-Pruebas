## Resumen

**BACKGROUND:** Neurofilament light chain (NfL) is an established biomarker for amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS). However, it remains unclear whether biomarkers reflecting amyloid-&#x3b2; metabolism (A&#x3b2;), tau (phosphorylated tau, pTau), and astrogliosis (glial fibrillary acidic protein, GFAP) can complement NfL in clinical applications. It is also unclear how Alzheimer's disease (AD) co-pathology affects disease progression in ALS. **METHODS:** We studied 264 patients with ALS and 164 disease-control participants, analysing baseline cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) NfL, A&#x3b2; peptides, pTau181, pTau217, and GFAP. A two-step diagnostic strategy was examined in individuals with intermediate NfL values, using machine-learning models. Prognostic associations were evaluated using linear mixed-effects and Cox models adjusted for established predictors; survival was additionally examined in pooled analyses including an external ALS cohort (n = 153). **FINDINGS:** Patients with ALS exhibited higher CSF NfL, A&#x3b2;38, A&#x3b2;40, A&#x3b2;42, pTau181, and pTau217 than controls, even within A&#x3b2;-negative subgroups. NfL discriminated ALS from controls with an AUC of 0.85 (0.82-0.88), which increased to 0.90 (0.88-0.92) in machine-learning models incorporating additional biomarkers. The machine-learning models maintained a high positive predictive value (0.90) for classifying cases with intermediate NfL values. Significant A&#x3b2; &#xd7; NfL interactions were observed for longitudinal functional decline, and pooled survival analyses showed stronger associations between NfL and survival in the A&#x3b2;-positive ALS subgroup (14%) than in the A&#x3b2;-negative ALS subgroup (HR 4.02 [2.17-7.45] vs 1.62 [1.36-1.92]). **INTERPRETATION:** Multiple biomarker assessments can reduce misclassification of individuals with intermediate NfL values. CSF A&#x3b2; positivity modified the association between baseline NfL and disease progression. Personalised diagnosis and prognosis based on fine-grained co-pathology evaluation may enhance clinical management of ALS. **FUNDING:** This study was primarily supported by JST SPRING (JPMJSP2125).
