## Resumen

**BACKGROUND:** Musculoskeletal disorders are pathological states in which a person has pain, inflammation, stiffness, and limitations of movements, which involve bone, joints, and muscles. They can also be age related, traumatic or immune system related and have a great effect on impairment of daily living and health. The Ayurveda in its holistic way of treating the body focuses on achieving the balance of body energies (doshas) in long-term wellness management. Basti Chikitsa, or one of its considerable therapies, is used in the treatment of the Vata of musculoskeletal and nervous systems. Combination of the ancient treatment with the current AI technologies opens new perspectives of individual integrative medicine. **OBJECTIVES:** The proposed study will explore the effectiveness of Basti Chikitsa in musculoskeletal disorders. It will also compare predictive models and AI capabilities of these disorders according to the Ayurvedic diagnostic principles. The ultimate objective is to create a smart product that will give Ayurvedic medicine and dietary guidance depending on the symptoms of the user. **MATERIAL AND METHODS:** The study commenced by collecting and analysing the dataset of the Vatavyadhi grading system and it involved 50 patient records. It consisted of the data concerning the disorders and such common signs as joint pain, swelling, and reduced mobility. The pre-stage of the data was done using feature selection, encoding and preprocessing. Data were divided into a training and a test set. Our experiments involved four machine learning models, namely, Random Forest, Support Vector Machine (SVM), Gradient Boosting, and KNN. Evaluations of models were based on their accuracy metrics and the most optimal models were chosen to be used in their further development. **RESULTS:** KNN and Random Forest were the ones that showed the highest predictive accuracies of around 98% and 96% respectively. They could be used successfully in the diagnosis of diseases like Amavata, Gridhrasi, Katishoola and Sandhigata Vata. The models also offered custom diet plans and Ayurvedic care recommendations, hence they were extremely handy with regard to integrated care. **CONCLUSION:** This study cites the possibility of AI to make Ayurvedic remedies of musculoskeletal diseases. We provide personalized therapy regimens, which are accurate and doshic-based, in line with machine learning and Ayurveda diagnostics. The trained models do not only suggest disorders with high accuracy rates but also recommend appropriate diet and treatment such as Basti Chikitsa. Therefore, this comprehensive system combines the wisdom of previous eras with the innovation of our modern age and can enable a more effective patient-centered approach to health and wellness.
