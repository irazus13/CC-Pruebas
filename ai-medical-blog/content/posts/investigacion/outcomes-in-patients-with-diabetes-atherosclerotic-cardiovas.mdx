## Resumen

**AIMS:** The impact of tirzepatide and dulaglutide on major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) and heart failure (HF) outcomes according to HF status is unknown. **METHODS AND RESULTS:** Patients with type 2 diabetes (T2D) and atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease were treated with tirzepatide up to 15&#x2005;mg or dulaglutide 1.5&#x2005;mg weekly (average 4 years). The incidence of MACE-3 [cardiovascular (CV) death, myocardial infarction (MI), stroke], MACE-4 (CV death, MI, stroke, coronary revascularization) and composite of all-cause or CV death and hospitalization or urgent visits for HF were analysed in patients with and without a history of HF. A history of HF (20.3%) was associated with more coronary artery disease and prior MI. Incidences of event categories were higher in the HF group. The hazard ratio (HR) for all-cause death or hospitalization or urgent visits for HF on tirzepatide vs. dulaglutide was 0.83 [95% confidence interval (CI) 0.70-0.99] in patients with HF and 0.88 (95%CI 0.78-1.00) without HF. The HR for CV death or hospitalization or urgent visits for HF was 0.85 (95%CI 0.70-1.03) and 0.96 (95%CI 0.83-1.12), for MACE-3 0.90 (95%CI 0.76-1.08) and 0.93 (95%CI 0.83-1.04), and for MACE-4 0.81 (95%CI 0.69-0.95) and 0.91 (95%CI 0.83-1.00) in patients with and without HF, respectively. The patient-reported outcomes of APPADL and EQ-5D-5L visual analogue scores improved and were higher on tirzepatide in both groups. **CONCLUSION:** With overall non-inferiority established, this exploratory analysis revealed lower rates of the composite of all-cause death or HF events, or MACE-4 among patients with T2D and HF treated with tirzepatide compared with dulaglutide.
