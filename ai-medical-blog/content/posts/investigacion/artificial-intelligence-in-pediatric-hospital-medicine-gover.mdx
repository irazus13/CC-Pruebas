## Resumen

Artificial intelligence (AI) is increasingly influencing pediatric inpatient care, extending beyond clinical applications to many areas of health care delivery, medical education, and research. Studies suggest that families are receptive to AI-assisted care if transparency, human oversight, and shared decision making are maintained. Effective governance is necessary to address concerns related to accountability, privacy, regulatory gaps, postdeployment monitoring, and equitable implementation. Algorithmic bias, data representativeness, informed consent, and the potential for AI to exacerbate health care disparities pose new ethical concerns. Explainable AI may improve transparency, trust, and acceptance by clinicians through making AI-generated recommendations more understandable. AI can also transform medical education through personalized learning, simulation, and clinical decision support, while requiring new competencies in AI literacy. AI offers opportunities to expedite clinical trials and data analysis but requires pediatric-specific frameworks to address age-related bias and emphasize ethical use of pediatric data in research. Responsible implementation, strong oversight, multidisciplinary collaboration, and continued clinician leadership will be essential to realizing AI's potential while safeguarding the welfare of pediatric patients and their families.
