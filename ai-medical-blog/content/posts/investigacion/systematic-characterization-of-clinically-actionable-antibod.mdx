## Resumen

The rapid expansion of antibody therapeutics is shifting the clinical bottleneck from drug availability to precise patient selection. We systematically characterize 87 clinically actionable tumor-associated antigens across solid tumors by integrating transcriptomic, proteomic, spatial, and single-cell profiles from more than 32,000 specimens to define expression prevalence, intratumoral heterogeneity, immune contexture, internalization and payload-sensitivity pathways, and translational relevance. We establish ANCHOR, a coordinate reference framework for standardizing patient RNA-seq data, and develop ReSort, a two-tiered scoring system for scalable target prioritization. ReSort enables virtual basket and umbrella trial matching, demonstrates concordance with clinical assays, predicts therapeutic response in patient-derived models, and identifies repurposing opportunities in rare malignancies. We develop an AI-based decision-support agent for antibody trial matching and construct a multimodal feature space integrating tumor- and protein-intrinsic attributes to guide systematic target discovery and prioritization. This work provides a scalable, clinically actionable approach for precision antibody-based therapies and next-generation target development.
