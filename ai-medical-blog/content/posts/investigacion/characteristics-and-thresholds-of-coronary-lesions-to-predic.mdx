## Resumen

**BACKGROUND:** Characteristics of future culprit lesions according to artificial intelligence-guided quantitative computed tomography (AI-QCT) are unknown. **OBJECTIVES:** The authors aimed to characterize and derive thresholds to predict future culprit lesions from coronary computed tomography angiography using AI-QCT. **METHODS:** A total of 2,169 patients/13,772 lesions undergoing coronary computed tomography angiography at Turku University Hospital, Finland, (derivation) and 553 patients/3,948 lesions from Amsterdam University Medical Center, the Netherlands, (validation) were analyzed with AI-QCT. Lesions from vessels undergoing early revascularization were excluded. Lesion-level total plaque volume (TPV) and noncalcified plaque volume (NCPV) thresholds were derived and externally validated to predict lesion-level and patient-level acute coronary syndrome (ACS). **RESULTS:** Future culprit lesions from both cohorts (n = 69 validation; n = 15 derivation) had consistently higher median TPV (101 mm3 validation; 115 mm3 derivation; P = 0.811) and NCPV (65 mm3 validation; 74 mm3 derivation; P = 0.709) compared with control lesions (P &lt; 0.001) and were nonobstructive in 69.6% (derivation) and 40.0% (validation). Thresholds of TPV &#x2265;55 mm3 and NCPV &#x2265;32 mm3 were derived and associated with significantly higher adjusted rates of lesion-level (TPV &#x2265;55 mm3: adjusted HR: 12.09; 95% CI: 6.95-21.04; P &lt; 0.001; NCPV &#x2265;32 mm3: adjusted HR: 10.82; 95% CI: 6.22-18.80; P &lt; 0.001) and patient-level ACS (TPV &#x2265;55 mm3: adjusted HR: 2.92; 95% CI: 1.65-5.16; P &lt; 0.001; NCPV &#x2265;32 mm3: adjusted HR: 5.11; 95% CI: 2.49-10.46; P &lt; 0.001) with consistent results in the validation cohort. **CONCLUSIONS:** Future culprit lesions vs nonculprit lesions by AI-QCT demonstrated significantly higher plaque volumes and were nonobstructive in 40% to 70%. Thresholds of TPV &#x2265;55 mm3 and NCPV &#x2265;32 mm3 were derived to predict future culprit lesions and associated with increased rates of lesion-level and patient-level ACS in 2 independent cohorts.
