## Resumen

Hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) represents a major global health burden with rising incidence, marked heterogeneity, and poor prognosis, necessitating reliable biomarkers for prognostic stratification. This study conducted a comprehensive multi-omics analysis, combining a large amount of RNA-seq data from TCGA-LIHC with a single-cell RNA-seq database. Feature extraction using autoencoders and 12 machine learning algorithms was applied to identify prognostic biomarkers, followed by survival analysis, single-cell atlas construction, and experimental validation. Using multi-gene representations from autoencoders, the authors determined that the PI3K/AKT pathway is a critical survival-related pathway. Integrated single-cell analysis uncovered 12 distinct cellular clusters, with the myeloid subpopulation showing pronounced transcriptional dysregulation driven by PI3K/AKT signaling. The intersection of 2,567 upregulated genes from myeloid cells and 105 PI3K/AKT pathway-related genes identified 24 overlapping candidates. LASSO optimization yielded a robust predictive model (ROC-AUC = 0.949), consistently identifying RAC1 and SLC2A1 as core prognostic genes validated in the external cohort GSE76427. Both genes showed strong associations with adverse survival outcomes and EMT pathway activation. Western blot experiments confirmed that RAC1 and SLC2A1 knockdown modulate PI3K/AKT pathway activity, while transwell assays demonstrated suppressed migration and invasion. This study establishes a comprehensive framework integrating multi-omics profiling, single-cell transcriptomics, and machine learning to dissect HCC molecular heterogeneity. This study identified the PI3K/AKT signaling axis as a key prognostic driver in tumor-associated bone marrow subgroups and identified candidate biomarkers RAC1 and SLC2A1, providing mechanistic insights for the development of targeted therapies for this challenging malignant tumor.
