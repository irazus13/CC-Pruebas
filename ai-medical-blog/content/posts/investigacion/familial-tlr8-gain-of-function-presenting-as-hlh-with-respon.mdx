## Resumen

Gain-of-function variants in TLR8 cause X-linked immune dysregulation characterized by cytopenias, bone marrow failure, and lymphoproliferation. We report an 8-year-old male in whom TLR8-GOF manifested as hemophagocytic lymphohistiocytosis, an association not previously described. Treatment with emapalumab was associated with clinical improvement.
