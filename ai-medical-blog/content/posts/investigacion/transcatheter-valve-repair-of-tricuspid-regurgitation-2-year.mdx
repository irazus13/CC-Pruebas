## Resumen

**BACKGROUND:** Tricuspid transcatheter edge-to-edge repair is a viable option for patients with &#x2265;severe tricuspid regurgitation (TR). **OBJECTIVES:** This study aims to evaluate 2-year outcomes with the PASCAL transcatheter valve repair system from the TriCLASP European, single-arm, postmarket clinical follow-up study, including outcomes stratified by baseline TR severity. **METHODS:** The TriCLASP study evaluated the safety and effectiveness of tricuspid transcatheter edge-to-edge repair with the PASCAL system. TR reduction and clinical, functional, and quality of life outcomes were assessed at 2 years. Outcomes were also assessed by baseline TR severity (&#x2264;moderate vs &#x2265;severe TR). **RESULTS:** In 300 enrolled patients, mean age was 80.1 years, 52.0% were women, and 75.8% had &#x2265;severe TR. Significant and sustained TR reduction was observed from baseline to 2 years, with &#x2264;moderate TR achieved in 81.3% (P &lt; 0.001). The 2-year Kaplan-Meier estimates for survival and freedom from heart failure hospitalization (HFH) were 77.5% &#xb1; 2.6% and 71.9% &#xb1; 2.8%, respectively, with a 71.4% decrease in annualized HFH rate (P &lt; 0.001). Patients experienced significant improvements in functional capacity and quality of life at 2 years (NYHA functional class I/II: 73.2%; 6-minute walk distance: +26.6 m; Kansas City Cardiomyopathy Questionnaire overall summary score: +7.3 points; all P &lt; 0.001). Outcomes were similar in patients stratified by baseline &#x2264;moderate and &#x2265;severe TR, including significant TR reduction and meaningful clinical, functional, and quality-of-life improvements. **CONCLUSIONS:** Outcomes from the TriCLASP study confirm durable safety and effectiveness of the PASCAL system at 2 years in a postmarket setting. Results demonstrated significant and sustained TR reduction, favorable survival, reduced HFH, and clinically meaningful improvements in symptoms, function, and quality of life, which were consistent across patients with moderate and severe TR.
